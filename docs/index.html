ClubOS

Run your student club without the group-chat chaos.

ClubOS gives university clubs one place for the things that usually scatter across group chats and spreadsheets: assigning tasks, scheduling events and meetings, posting announcements, running polls, and keeping a member directory — with a role hierarchy built for how clubs actually work.

Support

Questions, bug reports, or anything about your account or data — email basharkyousef2@gmail.com. We aim to reply within a couple of days, and to review reports of objectionable content within 24 hours.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account entirely from Settings → Delete Account inside the app, or leave a single club from Settings → Leave Club. No need to email us. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is removed and what stays.
